Mzansi 4th Division

The Mzansi 4th Division is a tough, hard-working league of community clubs from across the country. Money is tight, squads are thin and every point counts.

What it is like to manage here

Managing in the 4th Division is a test of resourcefulness. You will rarely have the cash to buy proven players, so free agents, loans and the academy become your best tools. A manager who identifies undervalued players and keeps the squad happy can win promotion with a team that looks unremarkable on paper.

Promotion and relegation

The top two clubs are promoted to the Mzansi 3rd Division, and in some seasons a playoff decides the last promotion place. The bottom two clubs are relegated to the Mzansi 5th Division. Every division has twenty clubs and a 38-match league season, with cups and other competitions played around it.

Local rivalries

Several places have more than one club in this division, which creates local rivalries:

  • Johannesburg: Joburg United, Germiston FC

Manager tips for the 4th Division

  • Look at free agents early in each window, before better-funded clubs move.
  • Keep the wage bill low and the squad small but balanced.
  • Do not ignore morale. A settled squad outperforms its ratings.
  • Treat promotion as a two-season plan: stabilise first, then push.
