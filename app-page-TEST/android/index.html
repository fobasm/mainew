Mzansi Manager for Android

Play Mzansi Manager as an app on your Android phone or tablet, full screen, with its own icon on your home screen. It is the same free game you play in the browser.

Download the Android app (APK)

About 10 MB. Android only. Free.

How to install

  1. Download the file. Tap the button above. Your phone may warn that this type of file can harm your device. That message appears for every app installed outside the Play Store. Choose to keep the file.
  2. Open it. Open the downloaded file from your notifications or from the Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the install. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. This is a one-time setting for that browser and you can switch it off again afterwards.
  4. Tap Install. When it finishes, open Mzansi Manager from your home screen or app list.

Good to know

  • Saves are separate. Careers you started in your browser are stored in the browser, and careers in the app are stored in the app. They do not share saves, so start your career in the one you plan to keep playing in.
  • Updates are automatic. The app opens the live game, so new features and fixes appear the next time you open it. Your phone needs an internet connection to load the game.
  • Landscape is best. Turn your phone sideways for the most room.
  • No account needed. Nothing to sign up for.

Prefer the browser?

You can play right now with nothing to install. Play in your browser. See the FAQ for more, or contact us if the install does not work and tell us your phone model and Android version.
