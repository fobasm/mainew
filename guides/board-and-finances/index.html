Board Objectives and Finances in Mzansi Manager

Winning matches is only part of the job. The board judges you on a set of objectives each season, and your club must stay financially healthy. This guide explains how both work.

Board confidence and job security

The board screen shows your board confidence out of 100, your job security and how many objectives are on track. Confidence rises when you meet your targets and falls when you fall behind. The board reviews your progress at the end of the season. If confidence stays low, your job is at risk.

Season objectives

Each season has several objectives. A league objective sets a finishing position, for example reaching the top half of the table. A cup objective sets how far you should go in the cup. A finance objective sets a limit on how much money the club may lose. Each objective shows a status such as on track, met or at risk, with a short note explaining why. The league note also compares your squad ranking against the rest of the division, so you can see whether the target is realistic.

Understanding the finance objective

The finance target looks at your projected result for the season. Wages and transfer fees count against income, so heavy spending can put the objective at risk even when results are good. If the screen shows a projected loss above the limit, you need to reduce spending or increase income.

Ways to improve your finances

Keep wages under control: avoid giving large contracts to average players. Sell surplus players rather than carrying a big squad. Check your sponsorship deals, since sponsors bring money in. Going further in cup competitions can also help.

Balancing ambition and safety

A strong team matters, but a club that spends beyond its means will face problems. The best approach is to set a budget at the start of each season, spend on your weakest positions first and keep a reserve. Then check the board screen every few matches to see whether any objective has slipped to at risk. Our transfer guide explains how to find good value in the market.
