Tactics and Lineups in Mzansi Manager

Your lineup is the single biggest decision before every match. This guide explains how to read your squad and choose a formation that gets the best from your players.

Reading the lineup screen

The lineup is shown on a pitch. Each player appears in his position with a rating next to his face and a position label such as goalkeeper, centre-back, full-back, midfielder, winger or striker. Higher ratings mean better players. Compare ratings across your lines. A very high rated striker is wasted if the defence behind him is weak.

Choosing a formation

A formation describes how many players you use in defence, midfield and attack. For example, 4-2-3-1 uses four defenders, two defensive midfielders, three attacking midfielders behind one striker. 4-5-1 packs the midfield with a lone striker. Formations with more defenders and midfielders are safer, while formations with more attackers score more but leave gaps. Choose the formation that suits your squad's strengths, not the one that looks best on paper.

Play players in their positions

A player placed outside his natural position performs worse. Check that every slot is filled by someone who plays there. If you lack cover in a position, look for a signing or a loan. Our transfer guide shows how to search by position.

Match preview

Before kick-off the game shows a preview with both teams, their formations and their lineups. Use it to compare your side with the opponent. If they have a strong striker, make sure your centre-backs are your best defenders. If their defence is weak, play your quickest attackers.

Keep your squad fresh

Rotate players when you have several matches in a short time, and check for injuries before each game. A well-rested second-choice player often does better than a tired star.

Quick lineup checklist

Is every position filled by a natural player? Is the goalkeeper your best? Are injured players out? Is the formation suited to this opponent? Does the bench cover defence, midfield and attack? Answer yes to all five and you are ready to play.
