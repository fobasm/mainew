Transfers in Mzansi Manager: How to Buy, Sell and Loan Players

The transfer market is where you turn an average squad into a winning one. This guide explains the tools the game gives you and how to use them without wrecking your finances.

Know your limits first

At the top of the transfer screen you see the numbers that control everything you do: your transfer budget, your spare wage budget, how many days remain until the window opens or closes, your squad size and how many foreign players you have. A signing can be blocked even when you can afford the fee, because the squad has a maximum size and a limit on foreign players. Check these before you shop.

The transfer window

You can only complete transfers while the window is open. When it is shut, the game shows how many days remain until it opens, and you can use the skip option to jump to the next window. Use the closed period to plan: build a shortlist, check which positions need help and decide who can be sold.

Finding players

The player search lets you filter by position, age, nationality, league and rating, and sort the results. Your filters are remembered for the career you are playing, so you do not have to set them again each time. Free agents are players without a club. They cost no fee, which makes them a smart way to fill gaps when your budget is tight, though you still need wage room for them. The scouting board and shortlist help you keep track of players you are watching.

Making and receiving offers

When you bid for a player, the other club decides whether to accept. Higher offers and fair wages improve your chances. The offers screen also shows approaches from other clubs for your own players, how many players are wanted and the best offer on the table. You are never forced to sell, so compare each offer with the value of the player to your team.

Selling and listing

If a player is surplus to requirements, list him for sale or open sell options from your squad. Selling raises money for your budget and frees wages. Do not sell a key player in a weak position unless you can replace him, because a gap in the lineup costs points.

Loans

Loans let you bring in players for a period without paying a full transfer fee, and let young players of your own gain match experience elsewhere. They are useful when you need cover for injuries or want to try a position before committing.

Simple rules for smart transfers

Buy for positions, not names: fix your weakest position first. Check the wage, not just the fee, since wages count against your finances every month. Keep your squad balanced, with cover in every area. Mind age: young players improve, older players decline.
