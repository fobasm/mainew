What is in the game

Five divisions, 100 clubsFrom the Mzansi Premier League down to the 5th Division. Two up, two down, every season. Live 2D matchesWatch the game unfold, change tactics, make substitutions or skip to the result. Transfers and free agentsBid, sell, renew contracts and use the summer and winter windows. Youth academyDevelop young players and promote them into your first team. Cups and AfricaDomestic cups and the CAF Champions League for the best clubs. Your club, your wayPick one of 100 clubs or found your own with a custom badge and colours.

How a season works

  1. Choose a club. Take a title contender or start at the bottom. See how to choose.
  2. Build the squad. Audit your players, pick a formation and fix the weakest position first.
  3. Play the matchdays. Press Continue to move the calendar and play each match live or simulated.
  4. Work the windows. Buy, sell and renew contracts when the transfer windows open.
  5. Finish the season. The review shows promotions, relegations, cups and your honours.
